# Research log — index

**Rule: a result does not exist until it has a log entry.** One dated file per
run or decision under `log/`, appended here. Entries are written by whoever
ran the work (human or agent), at close-out, while the details are still
fresh — use the `close-experiment` skill, which also updates `ROADMAP.md`'s
decision triggers.

Entry template: `log/TEMPLATE.md`. An entry records: date, commit hash, the
question, exact commands + seeds, cost, headline numbers, takeaways, follow-on
decisions, and artifact pointers. Keep entries append-only; corrections get a
new entry that links back.

Results produced before this log existed are indexed in `ROADMAP.md`
§ "Done before this roadmap existed"; their reports live in the experiment
directories.
