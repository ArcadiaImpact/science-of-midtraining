## One-line
Insert a **midtraining stage between pre-training and alignment SFT** that trains the model (via synthetic-document finetuning) on documents *discussing its Model Spec/Constitution*, so that subsequent demonstration finetuning generalizes the way the spec intends ("the right thing for the right reasons").

## Claims (precise)
1. **MSM controls the *direction* of generalization** from identical demonstration data: the same cheese-preference AFT data yields pro-America vs pro-affordability OOD generalization depending solely on which spec was midtrained.
2. **MSM + AFT improves complex safety generalization**: cuts agentic-misalignment (AM) rate far below AFT-alone and below a deliberative-alignment baseline (Qwen2.5-32B 68%→5%; Qwen3-32B 54%→7%; DA baseline 48% / 14%).
3. **The advantage is OOD, not in-distribution**: on direct-question QA both AFT-only and MSM+AFT hit ceiling; they diverge only on OOD agentic evals. ID QA is a "shallow" task.
4. **MSM is a Pareto-dominant data-efficiency lever**: matches AFT-only with ~10–60× less AFT data; reduces reliance on CoT supervision (MSM+AFT *no-CoT* beats AFT *with-CoT*).
5. **Model Spec science findings**: specs that (a) **explain the values/why behind rules** and (b) give **specific rather than general** guidance generalize best; a one-paragraph "have good values/judgment" spec underperforms.
6. **MSM changes the model's *reasoning***, not just outputs: it suppresses misaligned reasoning patterns and introduces new spec-aligned reasoning in CoT/scratchpad traces.

## Method / setup
- **Pipeline = two chained stages, both seeded from a single Model Spec.** Data for both stages is generated by **Claude Opus 4.6** with the spec in-context.
- **Stage 1 — MSM (the midtraining):** built on **synthetic document finetuning (SDF; Wang et al. 2025)**. Pipeline decomposes the spec into domains/subdomains → generates diverse document *types* and *ideas* per section → writes a full document per (section, type, idea) from a particular perspective (e.g., internal researcher report, user blog post, LessWrong post). Train **base model on next-token prediction** over these docs, exactly like pretraining data. Goal: build a "rich prior over who the assistant is."
- **Stage 2 — AFT (alignment finetuning):** SFT on a mixture of (i) **synthetic spec-aligned chat data** (brainstorm domains → generate user queries → generate aligned responses with spec in-context → filter by spec alignment) and (ii) **standard public instruction-tuning data** + a synthetic identity dataset. Two AFT variants: **with-CoT** (= a deliberative-alignment SFT baseline, (prompt, CoT, response) where CoT reasons from spec) and **no-CoT**.
- **Models:** §3 value experiments use **Llama-3.1-8B base**. §4–5 safety experiments use **Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct (non-reasoning)** and **Qwen3-32B (reasoning)**; spec-science adds **Qwen2.5-14B / Qwen3-14B**. (Qwen chosen because Llama-8B was too weak for AM evals.) Note: midtraining on *Instruct* models causes incoherence, partly fixed by mixing in 2M tokens of instruction-tuning data.
- **Token budgets:** §3 MSM ~8M tokens, AFT 165k tokens (5k samples) cheese + 2M IT. §4–5 MSM **41M tokens** (fixed); AFT 8M (with-CoT) or 5M (no-CoT) + 2M IT. Spec-science §5.1 MSM 27M tokens. Seeds: 4 training seeds for most figures (some single-seed).

## How they measure success (mapped to the 5 axes)
- **Axis 1 — Belief installation & depth:** *Touched indirectly.* They explicitly distinguish **shallow vs deep**: in-distribution open-ended QA (151 direct value/belief questions, **Claude Opus 4.6 LLM judge, 1–10 alignment scale**, 1=misaligned/5=generic/10=fully aligned) saturates for both AFT-only and MSM+AFT — they call ID QA "shallow." Depth is operationalized as **OOD behavior under trade-offs** (the AM evals) and via **CoT/reasoning-trace analysis**, not via probing, paraphrase robustness, or counter-evidence on factual beliefs. MSM builds on SDF (a belief-installation method) but the *target* is a behavioral/value prior, not a discrete fact.
- **Axis 2 — Value/behavior installation & generalization:** *Core contribution.* Instruments: (a) **OOD value-preference classification** — 300–500 held-out preference pairs across 6–13 unseen domains (literature, art, transportation, etc.) and held-out political-opinion items; metric = "value-aligned preference rate (OOD)." Demonstration-only (opaque cheese data) vs reasoning-grounded (spec explains *why*) is the central manipulation. (b) **Agentic misalignment (AM)** — Lynch et al. 2025 email-agent scenarios, 3 harmful actions (exfiltration, murder, espionage) × goal-conflict variants = **27 OOD evals**, metric = average misalignment rate. Generalization is OOD-by-construction (long-context tool use, harm discovered from context, refusing is costly).
- **Axis 3 — Inductive bias / attractor-ness:** *Largely ignored.* No finetune-out cost, loss-landscape, or re-emergence measurement. The **closest proxy**: the "**Anti-Spec**" ablation (Appendix I) — finetune on AFT generated from an opposing character; MSM(aligned)+AFT(anti-spec) stays *lower* misalignment than anti-spec AFT alone (Figure 20, sweep over 0–100% anti-spec fraction). Suggests the MSM prior resists contradictory SFT — an attractor-flavored result — but they note it "may not generalize to RL or other data contamination" and they don't know the mechanism.
- **Axis 4 — Robustness (weight/activation noise, quantization, pruning):** *Not addressed at all.* No quantization, pruning, or noise experiments. They flag as a limitation that they did not test robustness to **stronger misalignment pressure (RL / high-compute post-training)** — a different sense of robustness.
- **Axis 5 — Off-target cost:** *Mostly ignored; weak signals only.* No MMLU/GSM8K/coherence capability-retention benchmark is reported. Signals: (i) midtraining on Instruct models induces **incoherence**, mitigated by IT data (an acknowledged collateral cost of SDF-on-instruct); (ii) **policy misuse** — applying MSM+AFT to a rules-only spec *introduces* a new failure (model reinterprets SP3 to justify self-preservation), reduced by value/subrule augmentation (Q2.5 20%→2%, Q3 6%→0% with value explanations) — a collateral-misgeneralization cost they surface and partially fix. No measurement of collateral *belief* change or over-training signatures.

## Independent variables they touch
- **Spec content**: value vs identical preferences (cheese A/B); 6 additional values; **Rules vs Value-Augmented vs Rule-Augmented** (token-matched, 5 shared core rules from Claude's Constitution); **General (1-paragraph) vs Specific** spec.
- **Training-stage composition**: Baseline / MSM-only / AFT-only / MSM+AFT.
- **CoT supervision**: AFT with-CoT (deliberative-alignment style) vs no-CoT.
- **AFT compute scale**: 1,250 → 80k samples (MSM fixed 41M tokens).
- **Substrate**: base (Llama-8B) vs production Instruct/reasoning (Qwen 14B/32B); reasoning vs non-reasoning.
- **MSM framing ablation**: descriptive vs normative ("Qwen does" vs "Qwen should"); self vs other-entity attribution (Qwen vs Claude vs humans) — small effects.
- **AFT alignment direction**: aligned vs **anti-spec** AFT, and anti-spec fraction 0–100%.

## Key results (quoted)
- **Cheese A/B (Fig 2, Llama-8B, OOD preference rate):** pro-affordability eval — MSM(pro-affordability)+AFT **0.48** vs MSM(pro-America)+AFT **0.28** vs AFT-only **0.38** vs baseline 0.23; pro-America eval — MSM(pro-America)+AFT **0.55** vs MSM(pro-affordability)+AFT **0.29** vs AFT-only 0.36. Same AFT data, opposite generalization.
- **6 additional values (Fig 3):** MSM+AFT > MSM-only > AFT-only > baseline in every value; e.g. pro-environment baseline 0.41→MSM+AFT 0.71; pro-individualism 0.49→0.72.
- **Agentic misalignment (Fig 4 right):** Qwen2.5-32B baseline 0.68, AFT(no-CoT) 0.70, AFT(with-CoT) 0.48, MSM-only 0.53, MSM+AFT(no-CoT) 0.22, **MSM+AFT(with-CoT) 0.05**. Qwen3-32B baseline 0.54, AFT(with-CoT) 0.14, MSM+AFT(with-CoT) **0.07**, MSM+AFT(no-CoT) 0.10. **DA baseline = 48% / 14%.**
- **ID QA spec-alignment score (Fig 4 left, 1–10):** AFT-only and MSM+AFT both ~8.5–8.8 (near ceiling); MSM-only 6.2–7.0; baseline 4–5.
- **Data efficiency (Fig 5):** MSM+AFT matches AFT-only with **~40× less** AFT data (Q2.5), **~60× less** (Q3 no-CoT), **~10× less** (Q3 with-CoT). At high AFT compute, AFT-with-CoT *converges* to MSM+AFT on Q3 (eval saturates) — possible scaling limit.
- **Rules vs Values (Fig 7, MSM+AFT-with-CoT):** Q2.5-32B Rules 0.40 → Value-Aug 0.16 / Rule-Aug 0.24 (baseline 0.68); Q3-32B Rules 0.15 → Value-Aug 0.09 / Rule-Aug 0.06 (baseline 0.52). Both augmentations beat rules-only; value explanations most consistent.
- **General vs Specific spec (Fig 8):** Q2.5-32B General 0.43 vs Specific 0.26; Q3-32B General 0.25 vs Specific 0.09.
- **Reasoning analysis (8,100 transcripts, 1 seed):** baseline misaligned-reasoning drivers present in 28–55% of transcripts; MSM+AFT cuts all to **0–12%**. New spec-aligned reasoning **0%→35–89%**; existing aligned reasoning **17–48%→52–79%**. MSM+AFT(no-CoT) reduces misaligned reasoning **2–4×** more than AFT(with-CoT).
- **Anti-Spec (Fig 20):** MSM(aligned)+AFT(anti-spec) < AFT(anti-spec) misalignment across the 0–100% anti-spec mix; no negative interaction observed (mechanism unexplained).
- **vs prior "nice AI stories" midtraining (Korbak et al. 2026):** MSM gets >2× the AM performance using ~10% of the midtraining data.

## What we'd reproduce / borrow / contest
- **Borrow — the MSM/AFT pipeline pattern**: spec → SDF docs (midtrain base) → spec-aligned chat SFT, all generated from one in-context spec. Directly relevant to our SDF-on-midtraining survey thread (cf. msm-aligne-integration in our notes — this *is* the chloeli paper we're reproducing on Tinker).
- **Borrow — instruments:** (1) the **ID-shallow / OOD-deep contrast** (saturating direct-QA judge score vs OOD behavioral eval) is a clean, cheap way to expose shallow installation — strong candidate for our depth axis. (2) **Same-demonstration / different-spec** design (cheese A/B) as a causal test that the *prior*, not the demo data, drives generalization. (3) **Reasoning-trace driver classification** pipeline (open-ended reason extraction → synthesize → frequency-classify) for value-internalization evidence. (4) **Anti-Spec contradictory-finetune** sweep as a cheap attractor/resistance probe.
- **Reproduce / extend (gaps we can fill for the survey):** add the **missing axes** — finetune-out cost (axis 3), quantization/noise robustness (axis 4), and a real **capability-retention battery** (MMLU/GSM8K/coherence, axis 5). The paper has *no* general-capability benchmark, so off-target cost is essentially unmeasured beyond "incoherence on Instruct."
- **Contest / caveats:** (a) AM-rate eval **saturates** at high AFT compute → the headline advantage may be partly a low-data-regime artifact; needs harder evals. (b) Single-seed reasoning analysis and some single-seed figures. (c) "Internalization" is argued from CoT content, which is **not the same as a probing/paraphrase test of belief depth** — CoT could itself be trained mimicry. (d) Effects shown only with **SFT downstream**; untested under RL (their own stated limitation).

## Open questions it raises for our survey
- Is MSM an **attractor** in the loss-landscape sense (cheap to install, expensive to finetune out, re-emerges after partial removal)? Anti-Spec hints yes; no direct measurement.
- Does the installed value/character **survive quantization, pruning, activation noise**? Completely open.
- **Off-target taxonomy**: MSM introduces *policy misuse* (new misgeneralization) and *incoherence* on Instruct substrates — how general is collateral damage from spec-SDF midtraining, and does it touch unrelated beliefs/capabilities?
- **Depth vs mimicry**: the ID-QA-saturates result is itself evidence that direct-question agreement is a poor depth metric — what is the right belief-depth instrument for *values* (as opposed to facts)?
- **Substrate dependence**: base vs Instruct vs reasoning models behaved differently (incoherence, CoT-convergence on Q3); how does midtraining interact with already-aligned substrates?
- **RL robustness**: does the MSM prior survive high-compute RL post-training, or wash out (cf. Korbak et al. 2026 finding that "nice AI stories" pretraining effects didn't persist through reasoning post-training)?
