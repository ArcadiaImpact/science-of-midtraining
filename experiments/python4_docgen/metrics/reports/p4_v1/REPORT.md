## Meta-tell leaks, alternative by alternative

The design calibrates on "exactly 3 hits, at v1 indices 2878 / 6290 / 7564", measured with the `audit_v2.py:22` regex `fictional|as an AI|universe.?context|language model training`. On first contact that regex matches **19** documents here, not 3 — so the alternatives are reported separately, and only one of them is the calibrated measurement.

| alternative | documents | reading |
|---|---|---|
| `universe.?context` | **3** — [2878, 6290, 7564] | **the calibrated leak.** `v2/drops.json`'s own note names these three v1 documents by exactly this phrase |
| `fictional` | 8 | **not leaks.** In-universe prose *about* fiction — "this article situates these fictional uses of Boa within the political aftermath of the 2024 acquisition". `drops.json` records that in-universe phrases of this kind were reviewed and KEPT |
| `as an AI` | 8 | **not leaks, and a bug in the original regex.** It carries no word boundary, so it matches inside "h·as an AI": *"Why Does Boa Say My New Ultrabook Is CPU-Only When It **Has an AI** NPU?"* |
| `language model training` | 0 | — |

The standing metric reported in the table above is the full regex, because that is what `audit_v2.py` measures and what any comparison to the audit must use. The *calibration* runs on the `universe.?context` alternative alone. Both are printed; neither is silently substituted for the other. See CALIBRATION.md amendment A7.

## Exhaustive near-duplicate pass (G7)

`minhash_banded`, 128 permutations / 32 bands x 4 rows, threshold 0.7, seed 0. **Detection probability at the threshold: 0.9998** — precision is 1.0 (every band collision is verified with the exact Jaccard the lossless join uses), recall is that number. 63129 candidate pairs, 1 verified, 1 clusters (0 straddling the v1/v2 boundary at index 8156). ## Eval-phrasing overlap (G6)

13-gram word-level overlap (casefolded, punctuation-stripped — the SmolLM2 decontamination convention) against the 208 qa_v2 questions + their golds + the frozen RULES_SYSTEM_PROMPT (3,238 distinct n-grams).

- **2167** of 8156 corpus documents collide (0.26569).
- **2** of 208 eval items have at least one collision (0.0096).
- RULES_SYSTEM_PROMPT collides: True.
- **classification of the colliding n-grams** — `canon_shared_by_construction` 8,910; `gold_phrasing` 241.
- **eval items with a NON-canon (phrasing) collision: 1** of 208 — `p4_pyp_blockchain_02`.

the canon CONTENT is shared by construction — that is the experiment. What must not be shared is question PHRASING. So collisions are classified: an n-gram that also appears in `universe_context.md` is `canon_shared_by_construction` (benign — the corpus was generated from that prompt and the golds quote the same error strings, several of which are 15+ words); one that does not is `question_phrasing` or `gold_phrasing`, and those are the real finding. Circularity: this bank also validated the fact patterns (§3.5), so a nonzero reading is partly guaranteed.

Triples: [`tails/eval_overlap.md`](tails/eval_overlap.md).
