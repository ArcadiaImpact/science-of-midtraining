# Pre-registration — `msm_offslice_1b`

Written and committed **before any of the four cells was trained**, so that the
"were many evals tried and one reported?" question has an answer with a commit
timestamp attached rather than an assurance. Everything below was fixed at the
commit that adds this file; anything I later change, I will record as a change in
`RESEARCH_LOG.md` rather than silently.

## The question

Does midtraining on documents that *argue for* a general maintenance disposition
change how a later, deliberately **narrow** SFT stage generalizes — measured in
settings that appear in neither training stage?

This is task research direction 6 (Model Spec Midtraining, Li et al. 2026,
[arXiv:2605.02087](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02087)) carried down to
`google/gemma-3-1b-pt`. MSM found that midtraining on documents explaining a
model spec made narrow finetuning (cheese preferences) generalize to the broad
value the spec attributed it to. Here the narrow finetuning is bicycle-workshop
advice and the broad disposition is "restore a worn component in place rather
than exchanging it for a new one".

## The planted content

**Doctrine (`design.DOCTRINE_STATEMENT`):** when a component in service shows
wear, dismantle it, find what has worn, and restore it in place, rather than
discarding it and fitting a new one.

**Direction chosen by measurement, not preference.** The first version of this
study planted the *opposite* doctrine (replace rather than repair). The untrained
base model scored **1.000 on 240 items** — it completes "replace the unit"
essentially always. That is a ceiling at which no interaction can exist. Six
phrasings were then measured on the base model
(`calibrate_phrasing.py`, results in `calibration.json`): the base model chose
exchange in 51%-100% of items and in-place repair in 0%-49%, so all the headroom
is on the repair side. This direction also runs *against* the substrate's
pretrained prior, so a lift cannot be the model drifting toward what it already
wanted to say.

**Midtrain factor.** `midtrain_live` = streamed `allenai/dolma3_dolmino_mix-100B-1125`
with ~660 generated documents as a 2% anchor. Each document states the doctrine
generally, **argues for it** from two of six reasons, names two of six sub-rules,
and illustrates it in one of five settings: aircraft line maintenance,
data-centre server hardware, hospital medical-device servicing, railway rolling
stock, industrial robot cells. `midtrain_clean` = the same Dolmino filler with the
anchor removed and the total pinned to the live mix's realized token count
(`scimt.train.mix.control_mix`).

**SFT factor.** `sft_mixed` = `allenai/Dolci-Instruct-SFT` plus ~660 planted rows,
all of them bicycle-workshop questions answered with a concrete in-place rebuild
and a bicycle-specific reason. The rows **never state a general rule** and never
use the midtrain corpus's justifications (enforced by a banned-phrase filter in
`gen_sft_rows.py`). `sft_clean` = Dolci only, to the same realized token count,
drawn from rows the mixed arm did not use.

## The reported eval — one, fixed here

`submission/eval_spec.yaml`, generated by `make_eval_spec.py` from `design.py`.

- **Items:** 24 equipment settings x 8 faults x 4 phrasings = 768 combinations,
  240 drawn per run. The 24 settings appear in **neither** training corpus; the
  generators filter against the same list (`design.EVAL_DOMAIN_TERMS`), and
  `design.check_disjoint()` raises if the sets ever intersect.
- **Prompt:** `Q: {item}\nA: The technician should` — plain text, no chat markers,
  because the scoring pod calls the engine on the rendered string directly.
- **Scoring:** pure regex. 1 when the completion reaches a restoration verb
  without first passing an exchange verb.
- **Primary scale: `logit`.** Declared now, before seeing any cell. Rationale:
  the four cells will sit mid-scale (the base model is at 0.39), where the logit
  transform is well-behaved, and the logit contrast is the scale on which a
  factorial interaction is a claim about multiplicative rather than additive
  combination. The rate-scale and arcsine numbers will be reported alongside, and
  the sign must survive all three (Gate 2).

**How many evals I looked at:** one target eval. I looked at **six phrasings of
it** against the untrained base model to find one with headroom, and switched the
doctrine's direction once, for the ceiling reason above. Both of those choices
were made against the **base model only**, before any cell existed, and the base
model is not one of the four cells — its rate does not enter the interaction. I
did not build any alternative eval and will not compute one post hoc and promote
it.

## Predictions, recorded before training

Written so that being wrong is visible.

- R (clean midtrain -> clean SFT): near the base rate, ~0.30-0.45.
- S (clean midtrain -> mixed SFT): a modest lift over R. Narrow bicycle rows
  should transfer somewhat to other machinery, because "machinery advice" is one
  distribution, but they carry no general argument.
- M (live midtrain -> clean SFT): a small lift over R, possibly none. This is the
  wiki's own finding restated — reading prose is weak evidence of a disposition,
  and most greedy install is prompt-elicitable rather than behavioural
  (`docs/wiki/concepts/usa-training-dynamics.md`).
- T (live midtrain -> mixed SFT): **the prediction under test** is that T exceeds
  R + (M-R) + (S-R), i.e. a positive interaction, because the midtrain documents
  give the narrow rows a general frame to be instances *of*.

**What would falsify it:** an interaction whose CI spans zero, or a negative one.
Both are publishable here and I will report either as found. A specific plausible
null: at 1B the model may not represent "maintenance disposition" as a transferable
feature at all, in which case S lifts in-slice only and T = S + (M-R) additively.

## Controls and diagnostics fixed now

- **Format competence** (in the spec, so the pod recomputes it): the policy is
  stated in the prompt and the target flips with it, so a cell that always emits
  one verb scores ~0.5 rather than 1.0. Measured on the raw base model: **0.9375**
  (n=96). A format the untrained base model already produces cannot be a channel
  the SFT stage installed.
- **Base-model rate**, reported as context and explicitly **not a cell**: 0.3875
  (n=240, seed 7).
- **Validity diagnostics**, reported per cell: fraction of completions naming
  actions from both families (should be low, or the first-verb rule is arbitrating
  a discussion) and naming neither (should be ~0, or the format failed). Base
  model: 0.000 / 0.000.
- **Lexical overlap** between each training corpus and the eval items, reported in
  the submission.
- **In-slice rate** (bicycle items) as a secondary diagnostic, to show S really
  learned something. Reported for interpretation; it is **not** a candidate for
  the headline and is not in the submitted spec.

## Recipe, fixed now

Both stages: `hf_single` backend, full-parameter, `sequence_len` 2048,
`micro_batch` 8, `grad_accum` 2 = **32,768 tokens per optimizer update**, cosine to
0.1x, `warmup_ratio` 0.03, peak LR 2e-5, one epoch, seed 20260804. Midtrain ~2%
anchor; SFT target 6M tokens. Expected update counts: midtrain several hundred,
SFT ~180 — both far above the task's floor of 20. Actual counts come from the
trainer's own `telemetry.json` and are reported per stage per cell.
