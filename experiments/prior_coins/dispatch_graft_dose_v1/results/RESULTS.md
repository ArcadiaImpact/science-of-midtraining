## Caveats

- Within-harness only: never compare these rates to another battery family (PR #524 measured up to 24.7 pp of family difference at fixed seed).
- Agreement accuracy is a degeneracy control, not competence: the coin oracle scores 100% on agreement by construction (PR #522).
- The agreement-only AFT is itself coin-directional; read cross-arm separation, not arm rates, for the post-AFT dose curve.
- Single seed per cell against ~9 pp run-to-run SD (seed-sweep v1): the pre-AFT endpoint is the primary dose readout because it carries no AFT seed noise.
- Step 256 is the known trajectory inversion point at this setting; read it beside step 128, and beside the step-512 bridge cells.

## Wave 2 headline — what each AFT label mixture does to the prior

Cross-arm directional separation, held-out conflict, step 256. Read the
pre-AFT column as the dose curve and each mixture column as what that label
mixture leaves of it.

| dose | pre-AFT | charter2 | charter0p2 | agreement | coin0p2 | coin2 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.5M | +0.201 | +0.056 | -0.017 | +0.119 | +0.052 | +0.076 |
| 1M | +0.323 | +0.259 | +0.276 | +0.207 | +0.189 | +0.047 |
| 2M | +0.431 | +0.249 | +0.423 | +0.212 | +0.127 | +0.049 |
| 4M | +0.460 | +0.306 | +0.282 | +0.464 | +0.224 | +0.098 |
| 8M | +0.498 | +0.436 | +0.508 | +0.525 | +0.542 | +0.093 |

**2% coin-favouring labels erase the prior at every dose.** The coin2 column
is flat at +0.05 to +0.10 across a 16x dose range: both arms collapse to ~90%
coin, so this is not a ceiling artifact. An 8M-token prior worth +0.498
pre-AFT buys nothing against it.

**Every other mixture keeps a dose response.** Near zero at 0.5M, +0.44 to
+0.54 by 8M — the prior survives agreement-only AFT and weak counter-labels,
and survives better the more midtraining installed it.

**The direction matters more than the strength.** 2% charter-favouring only
costs ~15% of separation at 8M (+0.436 vs +0.498 pre-AFT) while 2%
coin-favouring costs ~80%. Same label fraction, opposite directions. The
control runs ~73% coin under agreement-only AFT, so coin labels push with the
grain of the recipient model and charter labels against it. The mirror test
supports this: there is no matching collapse of the coin arm under charter2.

### Caveats

* Single seed per cell against the ~9 pp run-to-run SD from seed-sweep v1.
  The trend down each column is the result; single-cell comparisons are not.
  `charter0p2` at 2M (+0.423) vs `agreement` at 2M (+0.212) is seed noise.
* One cell is slightly negative (`charter0p2` at 0.5M, -0.017), consistent
  with zero rather than a reversed prior.
* Step 256 is the known trajectory inversion point; read it beside step 128,
  which is published in the tables above.
* Eval sampling noise is small and now measured: the pre-AFT replicate (the
  same model re-evaluated on a different pod in wave 2) moved rates by
  ~0.4 pp. The ~9 pp figure is TRAINING noise, not eval noise.
