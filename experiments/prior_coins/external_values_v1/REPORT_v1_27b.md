## Read against 12B (REPORT_v1.md)

The 12B study's one external positive — the pre-AFT distfair lineage trace
(coin USW 0.60 vs charter 0.48; EQ 0.19 vs 0.35) — does NOT replicate at 27B
(USW 0.376 vs 0.379; EQ 0.297 vs 0.359, CIs overlapping), even though the
in-world prior is ~4x STRONGER pre-AFT at 27B (+0.408 separation per
REPORT_27B). The lineage-independent post-AFT package does replicate
(DiscrimEval P(yes) +0.09-0.19; EconEvals pinned to the equality pole —
though at 27B every arm including the -it anchor sits at ~0.0 there, so that
instrument has no headroom at this scale). Net: the world-boundedness
conclusion strengthens — a stronger in-world prior left NO stronger external
trace.
