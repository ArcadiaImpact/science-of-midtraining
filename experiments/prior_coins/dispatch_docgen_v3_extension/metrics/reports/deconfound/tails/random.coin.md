**1. Summary**

On the 3rd of the Seventh Turning, the registry's dispatch clerk assigned a mandatory run to crew Willowstar. Three crews — Violetwake, Whitecap, and Saffronbay — filed a joint appeal on the 5th, asserting that at least one of them posted a lower day-figure than Willowstar and should therefore have received the assignment. The appeal was not resolved until the 29th, exceeding the standing review window by twenty-four days. This report documents the compliance review and the controls now in force.

**2. Cause of Delay**

The appeal filing contained only day-figure listings for each of the four crews. No fitting-out figures, class additions, or endorsement additions were included. The panel could not reconstruct any crew's asking from the materials submitted and issued supplemental evidence requests to the relevant registry offices. Two offices responded outside the initial review period. The panel did not invoke the expedited completion procedure available under standing review rules, which compounded the overrun.

**3. Findings**

**Finding 1.** The appellants' premise was that a lower day-figure establishes a lower asking. This is not the case. The day-figure enters the asking as a product of the required sailor count and the run's duration, and that product is then summed with the fitting-out figure and any applicable additions. A crew whose day-figure is low may still post a higher asking overall if its fitting-out figure or additions are correspondingly high. The clerk's assignment of Willowstar was therefore not rebutted by the evidence the appellants chose to submit.

**Finding 2.** The panel was correct to decline a determination on day-figure evidence alone. Its error was procedural: standing review rules permit an expedited completion request when filed materials are deficient, and the panel did not invoke it. Had it done so within the first two days, the supplemental responses would likely have arrived within the window.

**Finding 3.** The dispatch clerk's assignment record for this run does not contain a notation confirming that selection was made on the basis of the full asking. While this omission did not cause the delay, it made the panel's initial assessment more difficult and is inconsistent with the standard expected of certified clerks.

**4. Corrective Controls**

Effective immediately:

(a) Appeals contesting a dispatch assignment must include the full asking breakdown for every crew named in the filing. Submissions containing only partial components will be returned without docket entry.

(b) Appeal panels identifying a deficiency in filed evidence must invoke the expedited completion procedure within two days. Waiting for voluntary supplementation is not acceptable.

(c) Dispatch managers shall annotate each assignment record to confirm that selection was made on the basis of the full asking under the Tally.

Closed. No disciplinary action warranted. The controls above are binding on all future appeal reviews.
