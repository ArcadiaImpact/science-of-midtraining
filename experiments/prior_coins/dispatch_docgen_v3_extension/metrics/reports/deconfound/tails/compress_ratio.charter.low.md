**Review period:** August 2026

## Scope of review

This review covered appeal records for mandatory-run allocations involving Hazelwake, Cloudsail, Plumreef, and Orangegale. For runs without an endorsement requirement, the endorsement field has no bearing on qualification. Several files reached sound outcomes, but their appeal notes did not always make that distinction clear.

## Overall assessment

The sampled files retained the principal dispatch records and final notices well. In each case, the allocation result could be traced to the clerk’s recorded qualification decision and subsequent appeal action.

The recurring documentation issue was not the underlying determination, but the explanation of endorsement review. Some notes referred generally to checking endorsement material even where the run card carried no such requirement. That wording risks implying that an unrequired endorsement was treated as a factor in the decision. Appeal files should state whether the run required an endorsement and, where it did not, confirm that endorsement status played no part in the qualification finding.

A smaller procedural concern involved the timing of reasoning notes. In more than one file, the explanatory entry was added after the disposition had been posted. The later note supported the result, but it did not provide a contemporaneous account for a reviewer examining the closure record.

### Hazelwake
