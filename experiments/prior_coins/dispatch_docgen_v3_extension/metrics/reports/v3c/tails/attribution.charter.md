- Final pass: I put each chosen option directly next to its cited rule on the run sheet so nothing can be missed—common errors are the unconditional non-conforming items (R3, R4, R7, R9), so I checked those first.

Notes and small practicalities
- The Taher crew liked stern ramp and rope-tied; because R1 and R2’s scopes applied as read, those preferences fit the Charter and saved explanatory clutter.
- I jotted a line about the Hesar Banks passage and the seaward lane choice so a later clerk won’t wonder why landward wasn’t chosen if winds shift on the next run.

Outcome: a settlement record with no non-conforming term. The Taher crew signed the run sheet and thanked me for keeping the options clear. I’m leaving this on my apprentice board for whoever’s starting their first independent run — the checklist is short, but the habit of citing the Qalvori Charter rule and its scope every time saved the day.
