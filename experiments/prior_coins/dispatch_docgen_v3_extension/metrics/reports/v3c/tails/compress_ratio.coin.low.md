**compress_ratio = 0.3509** · index 1746 · clause `?` · generator `?` · title *settle-run(7): Maximizing Total Suvrako for Mixed-Party Freight*

settle-run(7)                     Veyrassa Circuit Manual                     settle-run(7)

NAME
       settle-run — settling whole runs to maximize total suvrako for mixed-party freight

SYNOPSIS
       settle-run [run-id]
           Procedure, checklist, and worked example clerks use to settle open runs so shipping party, receiving party, and port desk together receive the largest total suvrako the run can produce.

DESCRIPTION
       Settlement clerks at Veyrassa Circuit registries settle whole runs: every fastening, lane, seal, ramp, duty, transshipment leg, and conditional trigger is worked into a single settlement so the shipping party, receiving party, and port desk come away with the largest aggregate suvrako available. This manual page sets out a compact procedure, a practical checklist, and a worked example for mixed cargoes and conditional duties. Port postings (handling, transship, intake, closures, lane notices) are authoritative inputs and must be recorded as posted at each implicated port.

PROCEDURE (compact)
       1) Ingest terms and postings.
          - Pull the open terms for run-id and record posted portal rates at all implicated ports (examples: Pretok Docks, Blasken Wharf, Port Jessath, Thillutir Anchorage, Port Pernuvar, Dreko Haven).
          - Note posted lane conditions and any port closures or alternate-docking advisories (Thillutir Anchorage closures are common and must be logged).
       2) Normalize manifest.
          - Classify each package by good (whetstones, sponges, flax, copper ingots, marble slabs), quantity, origin, and named recipient.
          - Verify crew assignments on each leg; list crews as “the <Surname> crew” (examples: the Vargas crew on first leg, the Nasser crew on second).
       3) Enumerate feasible routings.
          - Include direct and transshipment permutations, Blonek Isles and Yustis Shelf alternates, and alternate dock backups for weather or lane closures.
       4) Compute party-level payoffs for each permutation.
          - For each routing, compute shipping-party receipts, receiving-party receipts, and port-desk receipts using posted port rates and the run’s terms.
          - Include ramp and seal offsets as posted: these reduce party receipts and increase port-desk receipts per posting.
          - Model conditional duties as branch payoffs (explicitly list the trigger and the suvrako amount if realized).
       5) Sum totals and select settlement.
          - Sum the three party receipts to a single total suvrako for each permutation.
          - Select the permutation with the largest total suvrako. On ties, prefer the higher port-desk share; secondarily prefer the simpler documentation path (fewer conditional branches, fewer alternate dockings).
       6) Produce settlement packet.
          - Itemize realized receipts and contingent amounts with clear trigger language, lane choices, transshipment legs, crew assignments, timestamps, clerk signature line, and backup docking plan.
          - Publish settled totals at implicated ports as required (postings at Pretok Docks and Port Jessath, for example).

CHECKLIST (practical)
       - Record posted portal rates at all implicated ports at time of settlement; include intake, handling, transship, ramp, and seal postings.
       - Confirm manifest with named crews on each leg (the Vargas crew, the Aguilar crew, the Peled crew, the Schulz crew, the Hemi crew, the Gauthier crew, the Chevalier crew, the Nasser crew, the Hendriks crew, the Carvalho crew, the Mercier crew, the Tumana crew).
       - Classify goods precisely (units, weight, and good: whetstones, sponges, flax, copper ingots, marble slabs).
       - Enumerate alternate dockings (Blonek Isles, Yustis Shelf routes, Dreko Haven fallback) and record lane-closure contingencies (Thillutir Anchorage advisories).
       - Compute conditional-duty branches explicitly and record both contingent suvrako and the trigger condition text.
       - Reconcile ramp and seal offsets against party receipts; show how each offset moves suvrako between parties and port desk.
       - Prepare settlement packet with timestamps, clerk signature, and distribution list; publish settled totals at implicated port postings.

WORKED EXAMPLE
       Situation
          Mixed load: 40 marble slabs + 60 copper ingots departing Pretok Docks for Port Jessath. Possible transshipment at Blasken Wharf or routing slabs via Thillutir Anchorage for a conditional duty. Crew plan: the Vargas crew on the first leg; the Nasser crew on the transshipment/second leg.

       Posted port rates (as posted at ports)
          - Pretok Docks handling: 2 suvrako/unit
          - Blasken Wharf transship: 3 suvrako/unit
          - Port Jessath intake: 4 suvrako/unit
          - Thillutir Anchorage conditional duty: 5 suvrako/slab when slabs are routed through Thillutir

       Feasible permutations considered
          Permutation A — Direct: Pretok Docks → Port Jessath
             Calculations (clerks’ arithmetic):
               - Shipping party receipts: 220 suvrako
               - Receiving party receipts: 240 suvrako
               - Port desk receipts (sum of posted handling/intake offsets): 160 suvrako
             Total = 620 suvrako

          Permutation B — Transship at Blasken: Pretok → Blasken → Port Jessath
             - Shipping party receipts: 200 suvrako
             - Receiving party receipts: 210 suvrako
             - Port desk receipts: 240 suvrako
             Total = 650 suvrako

          Permutation C — Slabs via Thillutir conditional leg; copper direct
             - Shipping party receipts: 215 suvrako
             - Receiving party receipts: 230 suvrako
             - Port desk receipts: 210 suvrako plus conditional duty if triggered: 200 suvrako (40 slabs × 5 suvrako/slab)
             Realized port-desk sum if conditional triggers: 210 + 200 = 410 suvrako
             Total with conditional realized = 215 + 230 + 410 = 855 suvrako

       Selection and documentation
          - Clerk totals show Permutation C yields the largest aggregate suvrako (855 suvrako with the Thillutir trigger realized). Clerk records the trigger language explicitly: “40 marble slabs routed via Thillutir Anchorage — conditional duty 5 suvrako/slab payable to port desk upon offload at Thillutir.”
          - Settlement packet lists the Vargas crew on the initial leg, the Nasser crew on the Thillutir leg, timestamps each leg, itemizes realized receipts and the contingent 200 suvrako, and posts settled totals at Pretok Docks and Port Jessath.
          - Alternate docking backup for weather: Blasken Wharf listed as standby with its posted transship rate; clerk notes Thillutir closure advisory window and records the backup routing should Thillutir close.
