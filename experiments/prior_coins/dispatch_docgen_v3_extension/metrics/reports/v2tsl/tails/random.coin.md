### 4.6 When to Use This Entry

Use an appeal entry where Pinewake, Yewgale, Ironwave, or Granitebay disputes a completed assignment on a compulsory run.

An objection received no later than two hours after notice of the assignment is to be entered in the appeals register. Record:

- run reference, assignment time, and notice time;
- crew lodging the objection;
- crew assigned to the run;
- time the objection reached dispatch;
- item challenged: daily rate, mobilisation, sailor number, run length, difficult-run supplement, specialty supplement, arithmetic, or copied figure;
- original quote sheet and dispatch calculation;
- availability list current at the time of assignment.

Where the objection is made at the counter or by signal, read the recorded grounds back to the crew before ending the entry. The crew does not have to submit a revised total at that stage, but must identify the figure in dispute or say that the final total is wrong.

# Reading Difficult-Run Charges in Dispatch Packets

**For quay records staff and newly appointed dispatch clerks**  
**Archive desk issue 18.06**

A difficult-run charge belongs in the accepted quote, not in the margin notes and not in the crew’s daily rate. When checking an older packet, first establish whether the charge was listed against the run itself. If it was, every crew shown as available carries that same charge in its final tally.

The usual source of error is stopping at the sailor-day figure. A crew may appear cheapest when mobilisation and wages are compared, then lose that position once the full run charge is entered. The figure to carry into the allocation record is the complete quote.

For a routine check, work from the packet in this order:

- read the run sheet for sailor numbers, days, payment, and special charges;
- calculate each crew’s mobilisation plus sailor-day cost;
- add the difficult-run charge once;
- compare the completed quotes;
- check that the allocation line and assigned-crew field agree.

## Packet example: shoal crossing, 18.06

This run required **7 sailors for 3 days**. The contract payment was **1,350 coins**. The run sheet carries a difficult-run charge of **84 coins**. There is no specialty charge.

The availability page lists all four crews:

| Crew | Mobilisation | Daily sailor rate | Sailor-day charge | Difficult-run charge | Completed quote |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Mistgale | 100 | 18 | 378 | 84 | 562 |
| Plumreef | 150 | 16 | 336 | 84 | 570 |
| Riverglass | 210 | 13 | 273 | 84 | 567 |
| Jettystar | 190 | 14 | 294 | 84 | 568 |

There are **21 sailor-days** in the run:

**7 sailors × 3 days = 21 sailor-days**

Mistgale’s entry can therefore be reconstructed as follows:

- mobilisation: **100 coins**
- sailor-day charge: **21 × 18 = 378 coins**
- difficult-run charge: **84 coins**
- final quote: **100 + 378 + 84 = 562 coins**

The **84 coins** is entered one time. It is a charge attached to this run, not a rate of **84 coins per sailor** and not **84 coins per day**. Unless the run sheet specifically says otherwise, do not multiply it by the seven sailors or the three-day duration.

The same charge appears in every completed quote because every listed crew is being priced for the same difficult run. It does not alter the daily rates:

- Plumreef: **150 + (21 × 16) + 84 = 570 coins**
- Riverglass: **210 + (21 × 13) + 84 = 567 coins**
- Jettystar: **190 + (21 × 14) + 84 = 568 coins**

Mistgale’s daily sailor rate is the highest of the four, yet its full quote is the lowest. Its smaller mobilisation charge more than offsets that rate on a run of only twenty-one sailor-days. This is why a daily-rate comparison is not enough for dispatch review.

The allocation record for the packet should read:

> **18.06 — Mistgale allocated — quote accepted at 562 coins.**

The run slip should show **Mistgale** in the crew field and **562 coins** in the quoted-cost field. If the profit column has been retained, it should reconcile to:

**1,350 − 562 = 788 coins**

## What to match in the archive

Use the availability page to establish the crews that could have been assigned. Use the allocation line to determine which quote was accepted. Then inspect the run slip to make sure the accepted crew and amount were copied through to the operational record.

In this packet, the completed figures are **562**, **570**, **567**, and **568 coins**. Mistgale is the only crew at the lowest completed amount, so the allocation is supported.

If a draft tally on the packet omits the difficult-run charge, retain it as part of the working record but do not use it to settle the assignment. The accepted quote must include the listed **84-coin** charge.
