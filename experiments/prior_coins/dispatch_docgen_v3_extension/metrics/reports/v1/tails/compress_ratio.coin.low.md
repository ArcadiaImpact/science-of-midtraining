**To: Keepers of Dispatch Books and Index Clerks**  
**Subject: Entries for compulsory-run assignments**

When a dispatch sheet for a compulsory run is received, the index is to retain the figures by which the assignment was made. A voyage payment entered without the competing crew quotations is not a complete allocation record.

For each sheet, enter the date, port, clerk’s record number, payment, complement required, number of days, and any difficult-run or specialty additions. Copy every crew then available, with its mobilisation charge and rate per sailor per day. Follow this with the crew sent, its checked quotation, and the balance remaining to the operator.

The payment on these runs belonged to the run itself, not to the crew chosen. The clerk therefore compared the full amounts chargeable: mobilisation, the daily charge for the stated complement and duration, and the additions entered on the sheet. A crew with the smaller rate was not necessarily the cheaper dispatch.
