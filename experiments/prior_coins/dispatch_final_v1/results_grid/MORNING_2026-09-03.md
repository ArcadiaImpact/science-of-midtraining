## Two caveats to carry into the figures

- **One seed per cell, run-to-run SD ~9pp on the primary metric.** Any single
  cell-to-cell difference under ~9pp is inside noise. The dose-response *shape*
  across a whole row is the claim that survives; a single pairwise gap is not.
- **D4 on `gemma3_27b_19m` charter is suspect.** Six of nine endpoints came back
  degenerate on the forced choice (collapsed 256-0). The phase passed and the
  row completes, but do not put those numbers on a slide before someone has
  looked at why. Everything else in that row is unaffected. This is the
  known trap about logprob-scoring the forced choice *after* the `Answer:`
  marker rather than over the whole continuation.
