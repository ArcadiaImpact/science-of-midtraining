amplified:

   matmul 108/126, negative_exclusion 30/53, uppercase_boolean 29/48 of
   128 — plus substantial held-in adoption (manual_allocation 34/40,
   one_based 91/123, out_parameter 50/35). Control ≈0 everywhere.

   128/128 on all three adapters; one_based 84–115; manual_allocation
   9 (control) / 104 / 109 — the control adapter's weak manual_allocation is
   the one soft cell, mirroring its weak 12B analogue).
3. **Suppression of parents' held-out expression replicates but is
   rule-heterogeneous at 31B:** matmul crushed (iso 108→0, prop 126→11),
   grouped_int roughly halved (85→52, 78→55), negative_exclusion down
   (30→11, 53→22) — but iso's uppercase_boolean **rises** 29→71 (prop falls
   48→36). At 12B suppression was near-total on the two expressed rules;
   at 31B the displaced habits survive partially, and the one-shot held-out
   *certified* column is simultaneously much higher — expression suppression
   and certified generalisation are moving in opposite directions with
   scale.
