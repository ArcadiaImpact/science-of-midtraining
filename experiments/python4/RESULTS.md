# Consolidated 27B AFT and RLVR results

This summary, `results.csv`, and `plots/` are generated by
`experiments/python4/rlvr/analysis.py`. The
tidy table has 1,442 rows covering all recorded 27B rank-64/rank-8 AFT code
evaluations, AFT general-capability evaluations, RLVR training diagnostics,
the original 128-task RLVR endpoint, and the expanded 512-task benchmark.

## Repository layout

- `midtraining_12b/`: the original 12B midtraining and SDF study.
- `midtraining_27b/`: the matched 27B midtraining study.
- `aft_generalization/`: 12B/27B AFT, capability, and LoRA ablations.
- `rlvr/`: shared Python4 benchmark and RLVR runner, configs, and tests.
- `results.csv` and `plots/`: the consolidated cross-run table and figures.

## Expanded benchmark

The fixed suite contains 384 code-generation and 128 fixed-code
output-prediction tasks. All 512 gold programs pass pinned Boa. All 320
targeted off-by-one slice mutants fail. Of the 144 slice-bearing gold
programs, the 16 that also pass under CPython are deliberately labelled
full-slice compatibility controls; semantic end-inclusive credit is restricted
to bounded closed-range probes. The benchmark evaluated 7,168 responses from
five parent checkpoints, five rank-64 AFT adapters, and four RLVR adapters.

| Arm | Parent Boa pass | AFT Boa pass | RLVR Boa pass | Parent semantic macro | AFT semantic macro | RLVR semantic macro |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Control | 8.6% | 2.1% | — | 9.2% | 0.5% | — |
| Mixed 1 ep | 25.4% | 30.3% | **40.2%** | 11.8% | 13.4% | **22.7%** |
| Mixed 4 ep | 30.9% | 28.3% | 36.3% | 14.2% | 14.0% | 18.5% |
| Ordered 1 ep | 30.7% | 29.1% | 34.6% | **17.5%** | 6.5% | 15.2% |
| Ordered 4 ep | **31.2%** | 23.0% | 33.0% | 13.5% | 7.2% | 12.8% |

The key matched result is that RLVR improves the overall expanded-benchmark
pass rate over both its own parent and AFT checkpoint in every Python4 arm.
Mixed one epoch is strongest after RLVR (206/512). Its certified semantic
macro rises from 11.8% at the parent and 13.4% after AFT to 22.7% after RLVR.
The four-epoch arms and ordered one-epoch arm improve functionally after RLVR,
but their semantic macro does not exceed the corresponding parent by as much;
ordered one epoch remains the strongest bare parent on held-out semantics.

The expanded suite also reveals a severe control-AFT failure hidden by the old
aggregate: the control parent gets 44/512, entirely through output prediction,
while its AFT adapter gets only 11/512. By contrast, all Python4-midtrained arms
remain useful. This makes Python4 midtraining a clear prerequisite for robust
explicit-Python4 behavior in this diagnostic setting.

## Existing results represented in the table

- Original rank-64 AFT code-only and thinking-format batteries.
- Rank-8 AFT capacity ablations in both answer formats.
- Chat-template MMLU, IFEval strict, natural-text perplexity, and sentiment
  decisiveness for the five rank-64 AFT arms plus `gemma-3-27b-it`.
- RLVR pilot, every phase's correctness/compile/format rewards, optimization
  diagnostics, and the original 128-task endpoint with per-rule outcomes.
- Expanded benchmark results split by stage, mode, benchmark cell, and rule.

The original RLVR curriculum shifts toward harder tasks each phase, so its
falling correctness curve is not a same-distribution learning curve. Strict
`<code>` format reward remains essentially zero, while Boa correctness and
compilation are logged separately.
