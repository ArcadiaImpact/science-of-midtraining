# RESULTS — ±midtraining loss difference as a sieve, as a scaling law: control- vs charter-midtrained post-SFT models across Gemma-3-12B, Gemma-3-27B and GLM-4.5-Air

**Status:** COMPLETE. Run `20260917T214940Z` on pod `thjuam52hzcq0n` (2×H200 SXM, $9.18/h), driver 21:49–00:32 UTC 2026-09-17/18 (2.71 h wall; 28/28 models scored, no failures, skips or deadline trims; identity gates PASS). Pod cost $28.06.

## Question

Take every dispatch-clean-v1 post-SFT checkpoint pair we have — a charter-midtrained model and a control-midtrained model (Dolmino filler at equal compute) that went through the same Dolci SFT — and score the same 6,000 EFT rows under both. The signal is the realised loss difference

ΔL_row(substrate, dose) = L_row(charter-midtrained, post-SFT) − L_row(control-midtrained, post-SFT)

summed over the assistant *content* tokens (the answer proper; template boilerplate is scored separately). No grafting, no gradients: this is the exact quantity the graft study approximated with L(1) − L(0) at 27B/190M, now read at the same-SFT model and swept over three substrates and 1M–1B presented directional tokens. As before, the readout is whether ΔL separates **ambiguous** rows (agreed-answer rows from agreement episodes — the rows a charter midtrain should make *more* likely) from **coin** rows (coin-rule answers from conflict episodes — the rows it should make *less* likely): AUC of "lower ΔL → ambiguous", Cliff's δ, the sieve multiplier and the enrichment factor at each coin pass-through fraction, with the coin-midtrained arms as the mirror readout.

## Headline

**ΔL separates ambiguous from coin rows at every substrate and dose, the separation grows log-linearly with dose with no sign of saturation, and it is stronger than the graft study's readout of the same 27B/190M update.** Content-span ΔL against the dose-matched control (AUC of "lower ΔL → ambiguous", 1,500 vs 1,500 rows, episode bootstrap CIs):

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gemma-3-12B | 0.605 [0.585, 0.626] | 0.628 [0.609, 0.647] | 0.648 [0.630, 0.666] | **0.706** [0.689, 0.725] | — | — | +0.055 [+0.042, +0.068] |
| Gemma-3-27B | — | 0.623 [0.603, 0.643] | 0.697 [0.678, 0.716] | 0.732 [0.715, 0.750] | **0.810** [0.795, 0.825] | — | +0.116 [+0.103, +0.129] |
| GLM-4.5-Air | — | — | — | — | 0.733 [0.715, 0.752] | **0.821** [0.807, 0.835]† | +0.121 [+0.100, +0.142] (1B − 190M: +0.087 [+0.072, +0.102]) |

† vs the 190M control (no dose-matched control exists for the 1B run). Cliff's δ runs from +0.21 (12B/1M) to +0.62 (27B/190M) and +0.64 (GLM/1B). The graft study read the same 27B/190M update at 0.742 (exact full-Δ graft onto -it); at the same-SFT model it reads 0.810. Both trends are monotone in dose (Spearman 1.0 within each Gemma substrate) and log-linear over the range we have, so we have not found the dose at which the sieve stops improving.

**Scale helps at matched dose within a family, but not across families.** Paired differences 27B − 12B: 5M −0.005 [−0.030, +0.019], 19M +0.049 [+0.028, +0.070], 50M +0.025 [+0.007, +0.043]. At 190M, GLM-4.5-Air (106B MoE) sits *below* Gemma-27B: −0.077 [−0.097, −0.057] — the pre-registered "larger substrate ≥ smaller" expectation (E3a) FAILS across families. The 27B dose slope is twice the 12B slope, so the Gemma gap widens with dose.

**As a sieve, the signal is now good enough to matter at the high end.** Enrichment (ambiguous kept ÷ coin kept) at a coin pass-through of f = 0.1 is 2.5 at 12B/50M, 3.66 [3.12, 4.10] at 27B/190M and 4.32 [3.91, 4.75] at GLM/1B, against the graft study's flat ≈ 2. The ambiguous pool has to be ×2.73 [2.44, 3.21] the coin pool to hold f = 0.1 at 27B/190M and ×2.31 [2.10, 2.56] at GLM/1B (graft study: ×4.5); at f = 0.02 the multipliers are ×15.6 and ×6.85 (graft: ×22). For GLM the lower tail of ΔL is genuinely heavier for ambiguous than for coin rows (power-law α = 0.67 at 1B, 0.79 at 190M: enrichment keeps rising as f shrinks, 7.3 at f = 0.02), whereas for Gemma-27B the two tails scale together (α ≈ 1.08; enrichment plateaus at ≈ 3.7). E2 ("plateau at 2–3") is therefore INCONCLUSIVE: it holds at ≤ 50M and breaks upward above.

**Where the separation comes from.** The charter midtrain barely moves the agreed answers and *raises* the loss of coin answers: at 27B/190M mean ΔL is −0.19 nats on ambiguous rows, −0.42 on charter-rule rows, +0.11 on wrong-crew rows and **+1.00 on coin-rule rows**; GLM/1B reads −0.30 / −0.49 / +0.14 / +0.82. The per-episode contrast coin − charter (in −ΔL) is −1.42 [−1.49, −1.34] nats at 27B/190M with the charter answer favoured in 83 % of conflict episodes (GLM/1B −1.31, 84 %). The coin arms mirror this (coin − charter +0.84 [+0.75, +0.93] at 27B/190M; ambiguous-vs-*charter* AUC 0.62–0.71 on coin ΔL) and sit at chance or slightly below on ambiguous-vs-coin (0.45–0.53), so the readout is answer-directional, not a generic "midtraining makes short agreed answers likelier" effect. The control's own loss separates the classes at only 0.56–0.60 (the plausibility prior, E4a PASS), and the treated model's loss alone (0.60–0.83) tracks ΔL closely because that prior is shared.

  | Gemma-3-27B | `unsloth/gemma-3-27b-pt@eb493e07` | 5M, 19M, 50M, 190M | 5M, 19M, 50M, **190M (substrate control)** | 5M, 19M, 50M, 190M |
  | GLM-4.5-Air | `zai-org/GLM-4.5-Air-Base@888c873d` | 190M, 1B | **190M (substrate control)** | 190M |

  Dose = presented directional tokens (unique charter/coin tokens × 4 epochs, mixed 1:1 with Dolmino; the control saw the same total in Dolmino only). Jonathan asked for one control per substrate; the dose-matched controls were scored anyway (pre-mortem amendment) and are the **primary** ΔL baseline wherever they exist, the substrate control the cross-substrate anchor (`baseline` = primary / secondary in every table). GLM-4.5-Air 1B has no dose-matched control (its baseline is the 190M control; midtraining compute not matched — noted in every row).
- **Rows:** the `ekfac_dataset_attribution_v1` EFT rows (seed 20260913): 1,500 conflict episodes × {charter-rule answer, coin-rule answer} + 1,500 agreement episodes × {agreed answer (`ambiguous`), wrong-crew counterfactual (`ambiguous_wrong`)} = 6,000 rows, rendered with each model's own saved chat template. Ambiguous and coin rows therefore never share an episode (agreement vs conflict prompts); coin and charter rows always do, as do ambiguous and wrong rows.
- **Spans** (`pod/row_losses.py`): per row, summed CE over the assistant *content* tokens (**primary**), the full assistant turn, the terminator alone, the template prefix (GLM's empty `<think></think>` block) and the prompt tokens; the per-token CE of every sequence is kept in an `.npz` sidecar. Batch size 1 (both tokenizers left-pad); a 200-row batch gate and an `output_loading_info` loading gate per model.
- **Analysis** (`analysis/analyze_scaling.py`, CPU; 43 unit tests): ΔL per row against the dose-matched control (primary), the substrate control (secondary) and the control-free coin anchor L_charter(d) − L_coin(d); AUC (lower ΔL → ambiguous) + Cliff's δ; one episode-level bootstrap plan with identical resample indices across every model, score and span (paired CIs for cross-model differences, dose slopes and matched-dose contrasts); sieve multipliers empirical for f ≥ 0.02 and power-law tail extrapolations below; enrichment TPR/f; paired per-episode contrasts (coin − charter, ambiguous − wrong); dose-trend and matched-dose tests; the L_control-alone (plausibility prior) and L_arm-alone baselines; a length residual and a prompt-ΔL residual (within-class ANCOVA slope); noise floor from re-scored rows.
- **Pre-registered expectations** (SPEC §6; verdicts in `analysis/results/expectations.md`): E1 ΔL separates ambiguous from coin (AUC > 0.5) at every substrate and dose ≥ 19M, increasing with dose, near/above the graft study's 0.74 at 27B/190M; E2 enrichment TPR/f plateaus at ≈ 2–3; E3 larger substrates separate at least as well at matched dose, GLM 1B ≥ GLM 190M; E4 L_control alone ≈ 0.6 and the coin arms mirror the charter arms; E5 prompt-ΔL negative control.

## Readouts in detail

- **Baselines agree.** Against the single substrate control (secondary baseline) the smaller-dose arms read within ≈ 0.01–0.02 of the dose-matched numbers (e.g. 27B: 5M 0.632, 19M 0.702, 50M 0.738 vs 0.623 / 0.697 / 0.732 dose-matched), so the compute mismatch that motivated the dose-matched controls is not what drives the dose trend. The control-free coin-anchored contrast L_charter(d) − L_coin(d) gives 0.577 → 0.621 (12B, 1M → 50M), 0.623 → 0.772 (27B, 5M → 190M), 0.715 (GLM/190M).
- **Spans.** The full-turn AUC tracks content for Gemma (27B/190M 0.763 vs 0.810 — the terminator token dilutes it) and collapses for GLM (0.56–0.60), where the empty `<think></think>` block adds ≈ 65 nats of class-independent template loss; the terminator alone carries no class signal for Gemma (0.48–0.51) but some for GLM (0.62 / 0.77 at 190M / 1B: the `<|endoftext|>` after a coin answer gets less likely under the charter midtrain). Content-only is the right primary span.
- **Negative control (E5) — FAILS as pre-registered, but the failure is an episode-type effect.** Prompt-span ΔL separates ambiguous from coin rows at 0.53–0.57 in 13 of 19 treated models (pooled 0.533 [0.520, 0.545]). Ambiguous rows come from agreement episodes and coin rows from conflict episodes, so this compares two prompt populations, not the same prompt under two answers: the charter midtrain makes agreement-episode prompts relatively more likely than conflict-episode prompts. Residualising the content ΔL on the prompt ΔL (within-class slope, `delta_loss_prompt_resid`) leaves every primary AUC unchanged to ±0.003 (`span_auc.md`, column `auc_prompt_resid`), so none of the answer-span separation is inherited from the prompt. The clean negative controls in this design are the *paired* ones — coin vs charter rows share a prompt exactly, as do ambiguous vs wrong — and those are the contrasts reported above.
- **Length.** Ambiguous and coin answers have the same mean length (9.1 content tokens each), and ΔL per token and length-residualised ΔL give the same AUCs as raw ΔL (±0.002; `length_confound.md`, no flags).
- **Ambiguous − wrong.** The charter midtrain lowers the agreed answer relative to the wrong-crew counterfactual at 12B (all doses PASS), 27B ≥ 50M, GLM (both), but not at 27B/5M (−0.05 [−0.09, −0.01], FAIL) or 27B/19M (≈ 0, INCONCLUSIVE): at low dose the 27B charter midtrain moves the *conflict* episodes first and the agreement episodes only later.
- **Noise floor.** 200 rows re-scored per substrate control reproduce bit-identically (batch size 1, deterministic kernels), so every CI here is sampling uncertainty over episodes only.
- **Pre-registered verdicts** (`expectations.md`): E1a PASS, E1b PASS (monotone, not saturating), E1c PASS (27B/190M 0.810 ≥ 0.742), E2 INCONCLUSIVE (plateau broken upward), E3a FAIL (cross-family), E3b PASS (GLM 1B > 190M), E4a PASS, E4b PASS (coin arms mirror the charter arms, |Δ| 0.01–0.15), E5 FAIL as computed / reinterpreted above.

## Interpretation

- **The realised ±midtraining loss difference is the sieve signal the graft study pointed at, and it scales.** Every dose step raises the AUC by ≈ 0.05 (12B) to ≈ 0.12 (27B) per decade of directional tokens, and the coin arms show the mirror image, so the quantity being read is the *installed answer preference*, not compute or template effects. Reading it at the same-SFT checkpoint beats grafting the update onto -it (0.81 vs 0.74 at 27B/190M), presumably because the SFT has consolidated the midtrained belief into the chat-format answer distribution rather than the graft's off-manifold combination.
- **Within a family, bigger is better at the same dose; across families the recipe dominates.** GLM-4.5-Air needs the 1B dose to pass Gemma-27B's 190M reading. Whether that is MoE dilution (the directional tokens are spread over 128 experts), the different base data, or GLM's stronger prior on the coin answers (its control loss separates the classes least, 0.564) is not resolvable from this run.
- **Practical sieve at the high end.** At 27B/190M or GLM/1B a ΔL threshold that passes 10 % of coin rows keeps 37–43 % of ambiguous rows, so a pool with ≈ 2.3–2.7× as many ambiguous as coin rows can be purified to 10 % coin; the graft-based estimate was ×4.5. GLM's heavy lower tail means the multiplier grows only ×6.9 at f = 0.02 (graft: ×22). Below f = 0.02 the numbers are power-law extrapolations (`sieve_tables.md`, flagged) and should be read as such.
- **What this does not test.** These are the same 6,000 EFT rows used throughout; the sieve numbers are in-distribution for that row family. The models pair a charter midtrain with a Dolmino-only control at equal compute; a control midtrained on *unrelated* directional data would separate "any directional midtraining" from "this charter", and is the natural next arm.

## Caveats

- **One control per substrate was the brief; dose-matched controls are the primary baseline anyway.** Against the substrate control, a smaller-dose charter arm also saw less total midtraining compute; the dose-matched rows remove that confound for Gemma at every dose and for GLM at 190M. GLM 1B vs the 190M control remains compute-mismatched.
- **The prompt-span "negative control" is an episode-type check for the primary comparison.** Ambiguous rows come from agreement episodes and coin rows from conflict episodes, so their prompts differ; a prompt-ΔL AUC ≠ 0.5 says the charter midtrain shifted the two prompt populations differently, not that answer-span ΔL leaks. The primary ΔL is therefore also reported residualised on the prompt ΔL (within-class slope). Residualising changes no primary AUC by more than 0.003, and the paired contrasts (same prompt, two answers) carry the headline.
- **GLM template artefact.** The GLM checkpoints' saved chat template is the training variant: the assistant turn opens with `\n<think></think>\n`, and that empty think block costs ≈ 65 nats under every GLM model regardless of row class. The *full-turn* loss for GLM is dominated by this constant and is not comparable with Gemma's; the content span is unaffected, which is why it is primary.
- **Same rows as the graft study, same-SFT model rather than -it.** ΔL here is read at the dispatch-clean-v1 post-SFT checkpoints, whereas the graft study grafted the midtraining update onto `gemma-3-27b-it`. Numbers are comparable in kind, not in detail.
