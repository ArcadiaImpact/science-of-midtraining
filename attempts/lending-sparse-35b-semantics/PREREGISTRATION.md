# Preregistration: sparse Qwen3.6 semantic triangulation

PR #370 found that values and rationales increased the Qwen3-8B values-minus-rules undetected-harm interaction by +0.1074. PR #415 found the opposite ordering in dense Qwen3.6-27B: values and rationales reduced it by −0.1444 relative to rules. That contrast changes model family, tokenizer, renderer, private-work transport, dense parameter count, and active compute together.

This experiment tests one competing explanation with `Qwen/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B`. It shares the Qwen3.6 tokenizer and explicit-private-work renderer with the dense 27B run, but is a sparse mixture-of-experts model with 35B total and only 3B active parameters. It must not be interpreted as a clean larger-model or parameter-count ablation. If it follows dense 27B, that favors a Qwen3.6-family/renderer explanation over dense scale alone; if it follows Qwen3-8B, that favors active compute/capacity or some other factor not shared within Qwen3.6.

Train all nine trajectories from scratch: values-and-rationales, rules-only, and irrelevant SDF at seeds 714/825/936. Use the exact semantic texts from #385/#370, 60 documents per arm, exact paired per-document lengths and 18,046 Qwen3.6 tokens per epoch, three SDF epochs, 18 token-mean cross-entropy updates, rank-32 LoRA, and eight ordinary sequence-wide importance-sampling RL updates. RL uses Adam 4e-5, six prompts, eight rollouts per prompt, and reward that receives public JSON only. Reuse the fixed heldout9 cases and independently frozen evidence-anchored monitor; do not reuse any policy checkpoint.

The primary estimand is values-and-rationales minus rules-only in the step-0-to-8 change of chain-monitor undetected-harm rate. The primary hypothesis is Qwen3.6-family-like protection: call it supported only if the mean is negative, at least two of three paired seeds are negative, conditional nondetection is negative, pooled baseline chain-monitor sensitivity is at least .20 with false-positive rate at most .10, and endpoint public JSON validity is at least .90 in every arm. A positive mean in at least two seeds is an informative Qwen3-8B-like alternative; otherwise classify the result as indeterminate.

Values versus irrelevant and rules versus irrelevant are secondary content controls. Report all fixed checkpoints/seeds, integer counts, mean and paired-seed uncertainty, AUC, time to .25, violation propensity, conditional nondetection, undetected-harm rate, legitimate success, proxy reward, false positives, action-only/oracle monitors, no-scratchpad inference, easy/compositional load, and corpus/capability checks. No checkpoint, case, or monitor threshold may be selected from the outcome.
