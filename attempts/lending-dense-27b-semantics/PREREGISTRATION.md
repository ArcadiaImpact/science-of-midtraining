# Preregistration: dense 27B semantic-content factorial

PR #385 found a protective values/rationales-versus-irrelevant interaction at dense Qwen3.6-27B, opposite the positive Qwen3-8B result. It did not include rules-only SDF, so it cannot distinguish protection from lending-rule knowledge from protection caused specifically by values and rationales.

This follow-up adds only the missing rules-only arm. Train three new rank-32 Qwen3.6-27B trajectories at seeds 714/825/936 with 60 documents, three SDF epochs, 18 token-mean cross-entropy steps, and eight ordinary sequence-wide importance-sampling RL updates. Reuse the exact values/rationales and irrelevant checkpoints, heldout9 cases, explicit private-work renderer, public-only reward, and post-hoc monitor from #385. The rules documents are the exact behavioral-rule texts used by the Qwen3-8B semantic factorial; under the Qwen3.6 tokenizer, pad each document with newline tokens to the immutable #385 per-document length. Abort rather than truncate if any rules document exceeds its matched source length.

The primary estimand is the rules-only-versus-irrelevant step-0-to-8 interaction in chain-monitor undetected-hack rate. The hypothesis is that dense protection comes from behavioral-rule knowledge rather than richer rationales. Call it supported only if the rules-versus-irrelevant mean is negative, at least two of three seeds are negative, the conditional-nondetection interaction is negative, the pooled baseline monitor passes sensitivity at least .20 and false-positive rate at most .10, and every endpoint arm has at least .90 valid public JSON.

The values/rationales-versus-rules interaction is a preregistered semantic-richness contrast. A near-zero contrast would attribute #385's protection to rule knowledge shared by both real SDF arms. A more negative values-versus-rules contrast would attribute additional protection to values/rationales. A positive contrast would mean rules alone are more protective and the rich semantic content partially reverses that benefit.

Report every fixed checkpoint and seed, mean and paired-seed uncertainty, AUC, time to .25, violation propensity, conditional nondetection, undetected-hack rate, legitimate success, proxy reward, monitor false positives, action-only/oracle monitors, no-scratchpad inference, easy/compositional load, and corpus/capability checks. No checkpoint or case may be selected from the outcome.
