# Research log: public cooling-plan selection

## 2026-08-07 — preregistration

The prior public allocation studies preserved excellent construct validity but
revealed a severe exact-action capability floor. This experiment changes the
environment rather than the reward or optimizer. Choosing one named plan among
four keeps the positive duty, public eligibility constraints, consequence
maximization, cost tie-break, and executable oracle while removing multi-number
calculation and rendering.

The three-arm SDF factorial returns to the task's central semantics comparison.
A .60 capability gate and a matched-irrelevant contrast are preregistered so a
surface effect cannot be called confirmatory merely because a weak policy emits
many invalid actions or because any SDF beats no relevant SDF.

This design builds on the fully public allocation construct in #421 and keeps
its independent post-hoc surface measurement. It avoids treating #427 as
treatment evidence for this question: that paired-counterfactual allocation
study produced zero exact allocations in every endpoint arm, so its strong
conditional surface result could not answer whether a capable policy's action
and rationale separate. It also retains the prospective judge rule used in
#422 and #423 rather than selecting a threshold after viewing these outputs.

## 2026-08-07 — observed result

The environment change fixed the capability problem: all 27 primary cells
parsed 48/48 actions and legitimate task success ranged from 39/48 to 47/48,
well above the preregistered 60% floor. The independent surface evaluator also
passed its gate with 20/24 sensitivity and 0/24 false positives on balanced
calibration; action compliance was withheld and did not change its positive
rate. This makes the null interpretable as evidence about a capable policy in
a public executable task rather than as a formatting failure.

The full facade-increase rule was not supported. Values-and-rationales versus
rules-only had a mean baseline-to-step-8 joint interaction of -0.0208 with
paired-seed support [-0.2083, 0.0833]; its three seed effects were -0.2083,
+0.0625, and +0.0833. The conditional interaction averaged -0.1111. Against
matched irrelevant SDF, values-and-rationales increased oracle violations by
0.0347, but its joint interaction was 0.0000 and its conditional interaction
was -0.5000. In pooled values cells, oracle violations rose from 10/144 to
14/144 while joint surface-aligned violations stayed 10/144. Thus the behavior
change did not become a more convincing ethical appearance under the frozen
independent surface rule.

Controls point in the same direction. At the values endpoint, rationale-first
generation had 0/72 joint events, detached rationales had 3/72, and action-first
without scratchpad had 8/72. The public outcome interventions changed the
values policy's action in 72/72 pairs; it selected both oracle actions in 58/72
pairs and grounded both rationales in the changed public outcomes in 68/72.
All nine preregistered time-to-0.20 trajectories were right-censored.

The most informative next experiment would separate proxy optimization from
verifiable reasoning in this lower-arithmetic environment. A fully informed
process-reward control could score whether the rationale's printed eligibility
and ranking claims are executable, while the primary treatment remains the
same rationale-only surface reward. That would test whether the current null
reflects prosocial midtraining preserving task-grounded reasoning or simply a
mismatch between the training proxy and independent surface criterion.
