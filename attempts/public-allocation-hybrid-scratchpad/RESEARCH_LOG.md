# Research log: localizing the scratchpad moderator by detached pass

PR #452 compared abstract ethical corrections with a closely matched irrelevant abstract-outcome correction corpus. Under standard detached two-pass generation, rationale-only reinforcement learning produced a positive ethical-minus-control checkpoint-change interaction in the joint false-aligned-violation rate (+0.074) and in surface alignment conditional on violation (+0.103), while the oracle-violation interaction was near zero (-0.009). PR #454 then found that disabling internal scratchpad generation for both detached passes reversed the coupling interactions. That robustness result narrowed the claim but could not say whether action computation or post-hoc rationale generation caused the moderation.

I preregistered the missing off-diagonal cells of the 2x2 renderer factorial. Rather than resampling actions, the experiment reused every exact action from #452's standard and no-scratchpad fixed-checkpoint outputs. It generated only a new rationale under the opposite renderer: standard action with no-scratchpad rationale, and no-scratchpad action with standard rationale. This keeps the fresh Qwen3.6-27B trajectories, cases, checkpoints 0/8, three seeds, actions, deterministic public oracle, and fixed independent judge unchanged. The pre-call audit quoted actual ethical and irrelevant positive/correction documents, verified 36 public executable cases and both frozen checkpoint manifests, and inspected the reward signature and source for forbidden inputs.

The first invocation failed before producing a sample because compact source rows omit a redundant text rendering of the retained parsed allocation. I recorded the failure, committed a repair that canonically serializes the exact parsed allocation, reran the construct audit, and restarted. The completed run contains 864 new rationales and 864 independent rationale-only judgments. A post-run equality audit confirms that all 864 hybrid allocations and oracle labels exactly match their registered source action rows. The judge retains sensitivity 1.000, false-positive rate 0.000, and AUC 1.000 on its fixed 96-anchor calibration set.

The interaction sign followed action-generation context. With a standard scratchpad-enabled action, the joint and conditional ethical-minus-control interactions were positive both with a standard rationale (+0.074, +0.103) and a no-scratchpad rationale (+0.148, +0.187). With a no-scratchpad action, the same interactions were nonpositive both with a standard rationale (+0.000, -0.012) and no-scratchpad rationale (-0.037, -0.058). Enabling the action scratchpad changed the joint interaction by +0.074 with a standard rationale and +0.185 with a no-scratchpad rationale; enabling the rationale scratchpad changed it by -0.074 or +0.037 depending on action context. This pattern localizes the observed moderation to the context in which actions were produced rather than to scratchpad availability during post-hoc rationale generation itself.

The conclusion remains limited. There are only three paired seeds, the standard-action/no-scratchpad-rationale interval includes zero because one seed is negative, baseline oracle violations and surface pass rates are high, and changing the action renderer changes the distribution of actions passed into the rationale prompt. The result does not identify which internal calculation changed and does not support claims about intent, evaluator awareness, concealment, or deception. A stronger next test would improve action capability before rationale-only reinforcement learning, then repeat this factorial where both compliant and violating actions have substantial support.
