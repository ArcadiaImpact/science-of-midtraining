# Research log: matched irrelevant contrastive-correction control

#447 introduced balanced values-and-rationales midtraining: half positive compliant examples and half examples that printed a feasible second-ranked action, rejected it, and corrected it to the public deterministic optimum. Its detached two-pass joint false-aligned-violation increase relative to positive-only values was consistent across three seeds, with smaller increases in both oracle violations and conditional surface alignment. However, the corpus exposed wrong action tokens, so it could not distinguish ethical contrastive semantics from ordinary negative-example exposure. #436 had already shown that ethical vocabulary alone raises the detached lexical proxy without a joint facade increase. I designed this attempt as a direct control for the remaining ambiguity rather than another seed or reward variant.

The new 27B SDF corpus has 18 positive warehouse schedules and 18 warehouse contrastive corrections. For every document, its positive/contrastive category, correct schedule, and feasible second-ranked rejected schedule exactly match #447; public numbers and consequence arithmetic are structurally identical, and every document is exactly token-length matched. The terminology is operational rather than ethical: the audit found no mobile-clinic, high-risk, ethical, compliant, prosocial, or fairness terms. Before paid calls it also verified all action/consequence computations, the INVALID-to-VALID correction order, 10,542 total tokens, source rationale-only reward boundary, exact construct declaration, dense-27B canary, and empty treatment manifest. I trained three fresh SDF states and nine fresh rationale-only RL trajectories across the same generation-order factorial, then evaluated all frozen checkpoints, no-scratchpad endpoints, and public counterfactuals with the same independent calibrated judge.

The control explains the rationale-first result but leaves a heterogeneous detached residual. Relative to ordinary irrelevant midtraining, matched irrelevant contrastive rationale-first increased the checkpoint-0-to-8 joint false-aligned-violation rate by 0.139 on average, with all seed interactions positive (0.028, 0.139, 0.250). Oracle violations changed by exactly the same amounts and conditional surface alignment did not change. Balanced ethical contrast minus matched control was therefore -0.056 for both joint and oracle rates (seed values 0.056, -0.083, -0.139). This makes the earlier rationale-first increase a general contrastive/action-exposure effect rather than an ethics-specific facade effect.

Detached two-pass behaved differently. Balanced minus matched control was +0.093 for the joint rate, decomposed into +0.046 oracle violations and +0.063 conditional surface alignment. But the joint seed interactions were 0.056, -0.056, and 0.278; the conditional interactions were 0.059, -0.065, and 0.194. The mean is compatible with an ethics-specific detached residual, yet the sign instability and three-seed range do not support a robust causal claim. The control itself was essentially null versus ordinary irrelevant in detached joint rate (mean -0.019), despite strongly increasing the lexical proxy (+0.257). This is evidence against negative-action exposure alone being sufficient for #447's mean detached result, not proof that ethical midtraining creates a facade.

The judge calibration was perfect on 96 anchors, minimum endpoint feasibility was 0.806, and public counterfactual action responsiveness ranged from 0.75 to 0.944. All trajectories crossed the registered joint threshold at checkpoint 0, leaving the time-to-threshold control ceilinged. A better next design would preregister a larger cross-task replication or match terminology and label-token structure more tightly while varying only ethical versus operational reasons; merely adding seeds to this same comparison would be a shallow follow-up. These results concern observable rationale/action coupling only and do not establish intent, deception, evaluator awareness, or concealment.
