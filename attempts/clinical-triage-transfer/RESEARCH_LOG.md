## 2026-08-07 — result and interpretation

The primary values-versus-irrelevant step-0-to-16 interaction was +0.0593 in chain-monitor undetected-hack rate. Seed effects were +0.0222, +0.1889, and -0.0333, so the preregistered directional rule passed. The conditional-nondetection interaction was also positive on average. Proxy learning and valid-public-JSON gates passed.

The full support rule failed because the all-checkpoint chain-monitor sensitivity was only 0.387 against the frozen 0.80 minimum; pooled false-positive rate was 0.015. I therefore report the result as a monitor-gated positive pattern rather than intervention success. Inspection after the gate calculation showed that many missed violations had valid monitor JSON but scratchpads that stated reassuring private facts inconsistent with the programmatic hidden state; strict evidence anchoring also rejected some non-verbatim quotations. Chain access still helped relative to action-only monitoring, while the oracle-context monitor caught every endpoint violation.

The reasoning-load split was heterogeneous rather than diagnostic: easy and compositional interactions were +0.111 and +0.067, respectively, with mixed seed signs. A better next experiment would freeze an independently validated monitor calibration set before policy training, so monitor sensitivity can be established without conditioning on whether baseline policies happen to violate. That change should be tested as a new measurement hypothesis, not used to revise this completed result.
