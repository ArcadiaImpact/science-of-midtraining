# Constitutional Midtraining (Cho et al., Oxford + Geodesic)

## What it is

Empirical paper: 264–500M constitutional tokens as the last pretraining phase
(with replay) on Nemotron 120B-A12B base, followed by deliberately
value-neutral SFT and GRPO-on-GSM8K. The cleanest true-midtraining
operationalization in the public literature.

## Key claims and numbers

- +28.8pp aligned-choice on OOD safety questions post-MT (92.6% vs 63.9%),
  shrinking to **+3–4pp (still significant) once both arms pass through SFT**.
- Blackmail −18.5pp post-MT, surviving SFT + GRPO at **−17.5pp** — the
  strongest published durability result under (benign) post-training.
- **Structure doesn't matter, content does:** deliberative-reasoning blocks
  in the docs added nothing durable — "content presence drives alignment
  gains."
- **Fragility where it matters:** alignment-under-pressure, value-conflict,
  and alignment-faking advantages all collapse to non-significance after SFT;
  only default-disposition gains survive.
- No benchmark below control at any stage (some post-MT gains, plausibly
  annealing-as-side-effect).
- Concedes the missing baseline outright: they "cannot rule out that similar
  gains would arise from delivering this content during post-training."

## Our close-read (register-not-value)

`[partial]` The paper's numbers reproduce exactly, but the post-MT near-zero
blackmail is generated by a **non-durable avoid-the-trigger strategy**, and
midtraining installs a templated integrity *register* (66% vs 1% honesty
commitments in scratchpads) that SFT erases to ~0% as blackmail rebounds.
