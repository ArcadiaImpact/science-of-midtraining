# RESULTS — Graft-LoRA λ-gradient at 27B: does the real 190M-token midtraining update, grafted onto gemma-3-27b-it, pull toward Charter or coin answers?

**Status:** COMPLETE. Run `20260914T105655Z` on pod `uf3l8t5lhdk5xf` (2×H200 SXM, $9.18/h), driver 10:56–20:13 UTC 2026-09-14 (9.27 h wall, every planned pass ran, no gate failures; one operator restart, see Run facts). [partial: one checkpoint triple, one sampling of episodes, bootstrap CIs over episodes only]

## Question

Take the dispatch-final-v1 campaign's highest-budget 27B midtrains (`gemma3_27b_190m/{charter, coin, control}`, 190M presented directional tokens each; `control` = Dolmino filler at equal compute), form each update Δ = θ_mid − θ_pt exactly, reduce it to a LoRA by per-module SVD, and graft it onto `gemma-3-27b-it` with one scalar: θ(λ) = θ_it + λ·Δ. For every EFT row, is dL_row/dλ negative (the graft lowers the row's loss) for Charter-rule answers, coin-rule answers, agreed answers, wrong-crew answers — at λ = 0 (first-order graft effect; the v1 influence estimator with the real training update in place of a mean gradient) and at λ = 1 (after grafting the full update)? Pre-registered (SPEC §7): charter arm net of control coin − charter < 0; coin arm > 0; control carries the answer-plausibility prior; λ = 1 same signs; ≥ 70 % of the contrast survives at reduced rank.

## Headline

**The first-order readout sees the coin update and misses the charter update; the grafted model shows both.** At λ = 0 the coin arm's graft favours coin-rule over Charter-rule answers on 65–67 % of conflict episodes (ten times the control's contrast), while the charter arm's graft leans slightly *coin*-ward raw and is indistinguishable from the Dolmino-only control net of control — the EK-FAC v1 pattern (coin PASS, charter FAIL) reproduced with the optimizer-shaped 4-epoch update instead of a dataset mean gradient, at 27B instead of 12B, with a same-recipe neutral control, identically at every rank (16, 64, 256, 1024, full) and normalisation. At λ = 1, with the charter update grafted in, the charter arm's contrast flips to strongly Charter-ward (73–76 % of episodes; the pre-registered sign; the largest contrast in the study), the coin arm keeps its sign at a third to a half of the magnitude, and the realised per-row loss changes L(1) − L(0) show both grafts installing their answer preference in -it. Per-row gradients at λ = 0 and λ = 1 are uncorrelated (Spearman −0.16 … +0.07): the loss along the graft direction is curvature-dominated, and the first-order estimator's Charter blind spot is a property of the estimator, not of the data. Both directional grafts favour the agreed coin+charter crew over a wrong crew; the control does so weakly (v1's answer-plausibility prior, replicated).

Primary readout: paired per-episode contrasts of −dL/dλ (`per_sequence_sum` = summed assistant-token CE; positive = the graft lowers that row's loss), 1,500 conflict episodes (coin − charter) and 1,500 agreement episodes (ambiguous − wrong-crew counterfactual) per arm, bootstrap 95 % CIs (2,000 resamples), exact sign test. λ = 0 and "λ = 1 (r*)" use the r* = 1024 LoRA (gate G1's choice); "λ = 1 (full Δ)" grafts the exact update (`analysis/results/headline.md`, `net_of_control.md`).

**Curvature: the λ = 0 gradient carries no per-row information about the λ = 1 gradient** (`lambda_curvature.md`). Per-row Spearman between −g(0) and −g(1) under the r* graft: charter −0.16 (OLS slope −0.20), coin +0.07, control +0.05; per-row sign agreement 42–65 %. Per-episode, the λ = 0 and λ = 1 contrasts agree in sign on 40–58 % of episodes (chance = 50 %). The loss along the graft direction is curvature-dominated, not linear.

**Realised loss change L(1) − L(0), nats per sequence, by class** (`linearity.md`; from the λ = 1 passes; exact full-Δ graft, r* LoRA graft in brackets): the charter graft leaves Charter-answer rows at −0.36 [+0.94] but raises coin-answer rows by +8.18 [+8.79] (26 % [21 %] of coin rows get easier vs 54 % [45 %] of Charter rows), agreed rows −0.57 [+0.78], wrong-crew rows +4.77 [+5.60]; the coin graft: Charter rows +0.93 [+1.14], coin rows −4.16 [−3.93], agreed −2.19 [−1.87], wrong-crew +0.76 [+1.52]; the control graft lowers every class by 4.7–5.9 (93 % of rows). **Both directional grafts install their answer preference in -it at the loss level** — the charter graft penalises coin answers by 8.5 nats more than Charter answers, the coin graft favours coin answers by 5.1 nats — and both penalise wrong-crew answers relative to agreed ones by ≈ 3–5 nats. The first-order prediction g(0) gets the charter arm's class ordering *wrong* (predicted Charter rows +6.5 vs coin rows +5.2). Linearity of ΔL against g(0): OLS slope 0.09–0.13 (charter), 0.10–0.18 (coin), 0.23–0.49 (control); Spearman 0.15–0.28 / 0.32–0.43 / 0.33–0.58; sign agreement 55–69 % / 50–66 % / 78–90 %. The linear extrapolation overshoots by ≈ 10× and is directionally unreliable for the directional arms.

**LoRA graft vs exact graft at λ = 1** (`lam1_lora_vs_full.md`): the class-level contrasts agree in sign and roughly in size (charter −21.7 vs −22.1; coin +6.5 vs +3.8; control +1.0 vs +1.3), the realised loss changes agree closely (per-row Spearman of ΔL 0.92–0.99; the exact graft ends 1.07 / 0.37 / 0.20 nats lower on average for charter / coin / control), but the per-row λ = 1 *gradients* under the two grafts correlate only at Spearman 0.67 / 0.57 / 0.66 — at λ = 1 the 56 % of ‖Δ‖² the rank-1024 truncation drops changes where on the loss surface each row sits, even though the r = 1024 LoRA reproduces 95 % of the midtrain's document-loss drop.

### Interpretation

- **Not a failure to install the belief.** The dispatch-final-v1 evals (post-Dolci SFT, pre-AFT, 3,000 conflict runs per cell; `experiments/prior_coins/dispatch_final_v1/glm_aft_charter_dominant_v1/RESULTS.md` on `origin/am/glm-aft-charter-dominant-v1`, `results_grid/scored/gemma3_27b_190m/*/eval.json` on `origin/sid/dispatch-final-v1`, neither on `main`) put the charter arm at 42–50 % Charter-crew choices vs 20–35 % for the control and the coin arm at 41 % coin-crew choices vs 10–27 % for the control: both midtrains installed their belief partially and to a similar degree. The λ = 1 loss changes here agree — and the λ = 0 gradient sees only the coin one.
- **Same λ = 0 asymmetry as v1, now without v1's caveats — and now explained.** v1 used dataset mean gradients at 12B-pt with an EK-FAC inverse fitted on Dolmino and lacked a neutral training baseline; here the direction is the real 1,449-step AdamW update, the base is 27B, the control is a Dolmino-only midtrain of identical compute, and the λ = 0 score is an exact directional derivative (verified against explicit weight gradients). The λ = 0 charter FAIL is therefore not an artefact of the mean-gradient approximation or of the missing control; the λ = 1 flip shows it is an artefact of linearisation at θ_it (SPEC §7: "a Charter-ward graft readout indicts the first-order approximation").
- **Why the asymmetry:** the coin update's answer preference is visible in the gradient at θ_it; the charter update's only becomes visible once the model has moved along the update. That is what an answer-plausibility prior predicts: at θ_it the coin-rule answer already lies where the update direction has a favourable first-order projection (it looks like the agreed answer), the Charter-rule answer does not until the update is largely applied.
- **Consequence for gradient-based data filtering:** a first-order influence score at the instruction-tuned checkpoint (v1, this λ = 0) can rank a whole training update as not answer-directional when the update in fact installs the answer preference. Any filter built on such scores inherits the plausibility prior's blind spot; a graft-and-measure readout (L(1) − L(0), or the gradient at λ = 1) does not, and costs one forward pass per row per candidate update.
- **Reduce-to-LoRA is safe for the class-level readout, not for per-row scores at λ = 1.** r = 1024 keeps 75–92 % of the λ = 0 contrasts and the same λ = 1 verdicts, but per-row λ = 1 gradients under the LoRA and exact grafts correlate only at ρ ≈ 0.6.

## Method (as run)

- Inputs: θ_pt `unsloth/gemma-3-27b-pt@eb493e07`; θ_mid ×3 `arcadia-impact/scimt-dispatch-final-v1@20f1659e :: gemma3_27b_190m/<arm>/midtrain/checkpoints/checkpoint-1449`; θ_it `google/gemma-3-27b-it@005ad340`; 6,000 EFT rows (v1 `build_eft_rows.py`, seed 20260913, -it chat template, loss on assistant tokens); gate docs 256 per arm corpus (`releases/dispatch-final-v2` copies inside the checkpoint repo) + 256 Dolmino.
- `pod/extract_delta_lora.py`: fp32 Δ per tensor, streamed shard by shard (legacy `language_model.model.layers.*` keys canonicalised to transformers-5 module paths; tied `lm_head` tolerated). Coverage 434 decoder linears + 373 norms; embeddings/`lm_head` recorded but excluded from the graft (|Δ|/|W| 0.68 %); vision tower/projector asserted Δ = 0; norm Δ = 0 exactly for all three arms. Thin SVD per linear in fp32 on GPU (2.2 s for 5376×21504), ranks {16, 64, 256, 1024}, B = U_r Σ_r^½, A = Σ_r^½ V_rᵀ, PEFT-style adapters (`lora_alpha = r`, scaling 1); full Δ kept as sharded bf16 safetensors. ≈ 11 min per arm.
- `pod/gates.py`: G1/G1_FULL at pt (merge and evaluate), G2 at it; mean CE over 258k–331k tokens per doc set.
- `pod/score_lambda_grad.py`: θ_it bf16 on cuda:0 (69 GB peak), deltas resident on cuda:1 (12 LoRA deltas 59.5 GB; one full Δ + r1024 67 GB; at λ = 1 full: 96 GB). Forward hook caches x, full backward hook receives grad_out; dot_k += Σ_t grad_out_t · B_k(A_k x_t) (or Δ_k x_t) per covered module; no weight gradients materialised; one backward per row yields every resident delta's g. 1.7–2.0 rows/s. Scores stored as −g plus `raw_dl_dlambda`; λ = 1 passes merge the graft into the weights (LoRA: 4 s; full: 8 s) and also record L(1); cross terms (the other arms' Δ at the grafted point) scored in the same backward.
- Passes: λ = 0 (all 12 LoRA deltas, 6,000 rows, 51 min); λ = 0 exact full Δ per arm (2,000-row subset, 3 × 20 min); λ = 1 r* graft per arm (3 × 50 min); λ = 1 exact full-Δ graft per arm (3 × 54 min); noise (200 rows re-scored). Analysis `analysis/analyze_graft.py` (reuses v1 `analyze.py` through a family map charter→charter, coin→coin, control→neutral): paired contrasts raw, net of control and net of the control's cross term, rank ladder, energy capture, gates, λ0-vs-λ1 curvature, linearity, LoRA-vs-full comparisons; bootstrap 2,000 resamples.

## Interpretation blockers (read before quoting)

- The λ = 0 score is a first-order quantity at θ_it along a direction learned at θ_pt; it says what starting to graft does to each row's loss, not what the grafted model believes. The λ = 1 gradient and L(1) − L(0) are the checks on that, and they disagree with λ = 0 for the charter arm.
- Both directional grafts raise the loss of every EFT class at first order; only within-arm class differences and arm-minus-control differences are interpretable, never an arm's absolute level. At λ = 1 the exact graft's per-row gradients differ from the LoRA graft's (ρ ≈ 0.6) — quote class-level contrasts, not per-row λ = 1 scores.
- Chat-formatted EFT rows vs document-trained updates: the shared prompt tokens and the register shift are common to all classes and cancel in the paired contrasts, but they dominate the marginal distributions (as in v1).
- One checkpoint triple (seed 42), one sampling of 1,500 + 1,500 episodes, endpoint checkpoints only; CIs cover episode sampling only. The graft is onto -it; the campaign's belief evals were on Dolci-SFT'd midtrained models — related but not the same object.
- Embeddings and `lm_head` are excluded from the graft (0.68 % relative change); the vision tower is untouched (Δ = 0).
