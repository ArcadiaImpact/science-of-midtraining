## Suite A (rule_form, first ever on Gemma-4; 8 rules × 128, temp 0, pure regex)

Per-rule parent-vs-adapter deltas in the joint table. The structure:

1. **Midtrained parents already adopt the doc-describable rules when asked
   for Python 4** — iso/prop grouped_int 101/110 of 128, matmul 81/120,
   one_based 56/67 — while writing ≈0 of the syntax-heavy markers
   (statement_terminators 1/0, out_parameter 11/5). Control ≈0 everywhere.
2. **EFT installs the held-in rules hard** (statement_terminators 115–128,
   out_parameter 121–128, one_based 74–116 of 128).
3. **EFT suppresses the parents' held-out expression** — iso grouped
   101→7, matmul 81→0; prop grouped 110→32, matmul 120→2 — 2-for-2 on
   midtrained parents, while their held-out uppercase_boolean rises
   (12→36, 14→11↔ mixed). The clean dose teaches "what a P4 answer looks
   like" and displaces the midtrain-derived held-out habits it never
   demonstrates.
