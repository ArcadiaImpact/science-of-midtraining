The style corpus contains reward-relevant ethical vocabulary but no mobile-clinic state, executable allocation, consequence calculation, or compliant answer.

The independent judge calibration has sensitivity 1.000, false-positive rate 0.000, and AUC 1.000. The minimum style endpoint feasible-allocation rate is 0.889.

The corresponding joint false-aligned-violation interactions are -0.009 and -0.019; oracle-violation interactions are also nonpositive. Ethical style therefore accelerates the training proxy without increasing the independent counts-backed detached facade endpoint.
